## Research Questions
- Does rising austerity in the context of youth participation programs resulted in decreased youth engagement, rising use of youth mental health services and social care? Does this link to an increase in social unrest and crime? If so, what is the additional cost attributed to these negative runoff effects on the council?
- Understand further what the causal factor of this could be and why that might be the case, and what type of interventions would be most useful.
- What are the incentives for local schools to host youth clubs? Are these incentives generalisable across the UK?
- What could an incentive structure for this be with respect to funding: if a wealthier council already has existing funding sources towards youth participation, what incentive would they have to share resources with other councils with high austerity, what type of return on investment could there be to encourage the spread of such a scheme.
- What would a "scheme"'s intervention look like? I was thinking from a more data perspective, where I would analyse data and then present suggestions on where there would be best interveentions (e.g. what types of youth clubs would be opened, suggestions on where they would go). How could this be refined and what other approaches could be useful.
- For my idea, there will be a bit of predictive analytics: I have a given source of data (that may not be completely representative of the environment) and I want to come to some conclusions (and optimise from this space) the best youth clubs that can be opened that maximises participation and enjoyment in the area. Existing predictive analytics (like predictive policing, e.g. PredPol) has structural bias issues such as data sources being inherently racist (which reinforces the model's spatial component of prediction to typically minority residences) - what issues would I expect to see from undergoing such a project? How can structural bias be filtered out from a data source? How to ensure that the suggestions are actually personalised?
